How the ranking works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category, taken from this very snapshot (a live token means a recorded, non-zero market cap). A category with fewer than three live comps borrows its family's median; a category with no family uses the global median of all live comps. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%. Maturity rises with the protocol's age up to three years. Breadth adds 3% per extra chain, up to four.

What is left out, and why

The board holds the 40 watchlist protocols plus the 186 rankable pre-token protocols below the $50M floor. The 63 above-floor rankable protocols that are not on the watchlist are left out on purpose: they are bridges, liquid staking and wrapped products — infrastructure, not launch candidates. A protocol counts as pre-token when it records no token at all (no CoinGecko id and no ticker); a null market cap alone is not enough.

Limits

The implied value is a model claim built from public TVL and market-cap data. It is not a price, not a valuation, and it does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Farm plans describe how a reader could qualify for a future distribution; they are not financial advice, and no step asks for money to be sent to anyone.
